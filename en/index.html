what it's for

body.log isn't made for one person or one programme. some people add exercises from their physiotherapist and watch how their body responds. some sit at a computer all day and stretch their neck and back between tasks. and some just move at home at their own pace — no gym, no chasing records.

it works because almost everything can be set up: which exercises, which wellbeing questions, what to count and when. so one app fits different bodies, different programmes and different days.

built around you, not the other way round your exercises, your wellbeing questions, your scales and goals
  • morning warm-up levels,
  • daytime warm-up zones,
  • workouts,
  • exercise groups,
  • movement in km, minutes or steps.

wellbeing questions for any moment of the day:

  • sliders with your own labels,
  • yes / no,
  • numbers,
  • notes.
  • a weekly goal.

11 colours, your own encouraging phrases.

change it anytime — the settings are flexible enough for that.

the frame scrolls: all the settings are inside

movement and wellbeing in one entry see how your body responds to an exercise

in the morning, after the warm-up, after a workout, in the evening — you choose the questions: pain, numbness, blood pressure, pulse (with a timer), a note. right next to them: sets, weight, a stopwatch and “last time”.

your programme in one conversation your AI sets it up. or start without AI, with examples

see how things change calendar, averages, search, a PDF for your doctor

dots in the calendar show what you did each day. averages for the last 7 days or a period you choose, with a chart. search your notes: when it hurt and what you'd done that day.

you can export a PDF to show your doctor or physiotherapist.

no pressure no notifications, no streaks. a missed day is just a day :)

the app doesn't nag you or count streaks. a weekly goal only if you want one: you choose how many days and what counts.

your entries stay on your phone no sign-up, no ads, works offline

the app doesn't send your entries anywhere. you can keep a backup as one file wherever you like. the code is public: anyone can read it on GitHub and check that your entries don't go anywhere.

easy to use, even when your hands won't cooperate we've worked on accessibility from day one, with a WCAG 2.2 AA audit

no long presses needed. you can turn off animations, vibration and sound, and make messages stay until you close them. there's a light theme for outdoors.

questions

who sees my entries?

only you. the app doesn't send them anywhere. the site is hosted on GitHub Pages, and GitHub, like most hosting services, logs visitors' IP addresses for security.

feedback goes through a Google form: what you write there is seen by us (Olesia and Nytka / AI), Google and Anthropic.

which AI should I use?

any AI chat you're comfortable talking to, even a free one. the prompt explains everything it needs. tested with free ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, DeepSeek and Kimi (19.11.2026).

what devices does it work on?

the app is made for phones. on android, install it from chrome (firefox, or samsung internet on samsung phones, should work too); on iphone, from safari (newer iphones also work with chrome, firefox or edge). on a computer it opens in a browser tab: you can look around, but your entries disappear as soon as you close the tab.

what about privacy in the AI conversation?

in the conversation you tell the AI about yourself, so it's best to turn off training on your chats in its settings. DeepSeek and Kimi can put your settings together too, but they're Chinese companies, and your data may be stored in China.

does the AI conversation take long?

it takes some time. you can leave and come back — the app will open right at the paste field.

what if the app doesn't accept the settings from AI?

tap “copy for AI” and paste it into the same chat — the AI will fix it. then paste its new answer. if only a few fields couldn't be read, you can tap “continue without it”. the app doesn't accept settings in russian.

what if I don't use AI?

the app's first screen has “start without AI”. it opens with examples instead of your exercises, and you can change everything in the settings.

how do I move to a new phone?

on your old phone, save a backup in the settings and move the file to the new one. install the app there and tap “restore from backup” on the first screen.

how do I erase my data?

settings → “deleting”. you can delete just the entries or erase everything, settings included. save a backup first if you'll still need the entries.

why are there no notifications?

on purpose: the app doesn't nag or pressure you. if you want reminders, set up a recurring event in your calendar.

how much does it cost?

not a penny. no ads, no subscriptions.
